We are given:

- A bag contains **4 red marbles** and **6 blue marbles**, so a total of **10 marbles**.
- Two marbles are drawn **without replacement**.
- We are to find the **probability that both marbles are the same color**.

---

### Step 1: Total number of ways to draw 2 marbles from 10

$$
\text{Total ways} = \binom{10}{2} = \frac{10 \times 9}{2} = 45
$$

---

### Step 2: Favorable outcomes (both marbles are the same color)

There are two cases where both marbles are the same color:

#### Case 1: Both marbles are red

- Number of ways to choose 2 red marbles from 4:
$$
\binom{4}{2} = \frac{4 \times 3}{2} = 6
$$

#### Case 2: Both marbles are blue

- Number of ways to choose 2 blue marbles from 6:
$$
\binom{6}{2} = \frac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15
$$

---

### Step 3: Total favorable outcomes

$$
\text{Favorable outcomes} = 6 + 15 = 21
$$

---

### Step 4: Probability

$$
\text{Probability} = \frac{\text{Favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total outcomes}} = \frac{21}{45} = \frac{7}{15}
$$

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

$$
\boxed{\frac{7}{15}}
$$

So, the probability that both marbles drawn are the same color is **$\frac{7}{15}$**.
