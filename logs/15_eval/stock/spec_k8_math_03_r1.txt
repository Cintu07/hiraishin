We are given the system of equations:

$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y = 13 \quad \text{(1)} \\
5x - y = 7 \quad \text{(2)}
\end{cases}
$$

---

### **Step 1: Solve the system using substitution or elimination**

Let's use **elimination**.

From equation (2):  
$$
5x - y = 7
$$

Solve for $ y $:

$$
y = 5x - 7 \quad \text{(3)}
$$

Now substitute equation (3) into equation (1):

$$
2x + 3(5x - 7) = 13
$$

Simplify:

$$
2x + 15x - 21 = 13
$$
$$
17x - 21 = 13
$$
$$
17x = 34
$$
$$
x = 2
$$

Now substitute $ x = 2 $ into equation (3):

$$
y = 5(2) - 7 = 10 - 7 = 3
$$

---

### **Step 2: Check the solution**

Plug $ x = 2 $, $ y = 3 $ into both original equations:

- Equation (1):  
$$
2(2) + 3(3) = 4 + 9 = 13 \quad \text{✓}
$$

- Equation (2):  
$$
5(2) - 3 = 10 - 3 = 7 \quad \text{✓}
$$

---

### ✅ **Final Answer:**
$$
\boxed{(x, y) = (2, 3)}
$$
