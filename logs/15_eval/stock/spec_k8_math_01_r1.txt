We are told:

- Pens are sold at **3 for $2**.
- Notebooks are sold at **$3.50 each**.
- Sam buys **12 pens** and **some notebooks**.
- Sam pays **$29** in total.

We are to find **how many notebooks Sam bought**.

---

### Step 1: Calculate the cost of the pens

Pens are sold at **3 for $2**, so the cost per pen is:

$$
\frac{2}{3} \text{ dollars per pen}
$$

Sam buys **12 pens**, so the total cost of the pens is:

$$
12 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{24}{3} = 8 \text{ dollars}
$$

---

### Step 2: Let the number of notebooks be $ x $

Each notebook costs **$3.50**, so the total cost of the notebooks is:

$$
3.50x
$$

---

### Step 3: Set up the total cost equation

Total cost = cost of pens + cost of notebooks

$$
8 + 3.50x = 29
$$

---

### Step 4: Solve for $ x $

$$
3.50x = 29 - 8 = 21
$$

$$
x = \frac{21}{3.50} = 6
$$

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

$$
\boxed{6}
$$

Sam bought **6 notebooks**.
