We are given:

- **Pens**: 3 pens cost $2 → So, **1 pen costs $2 ÷ 3 = $0.666...**
- **Notebooks**: $3.50 each
- Sam buys **12 pens** and **some notebooks**, and pays **$29** in total.

Let’s define:

- Let **n** be the number of notebooks Sam bought.

### Step 1: Calculate the cost of the pens
Sam buys 12 pens.

Cost of 1 pen = $2 ÷ 3 = $2/3

So, cost of 12 pens = 12 × (2/3) = $8

### Step 2: Set up the total cost equation
Total cost = cost of pens + cost of notebooks

$$
\text{Total cost} = 8 + 3.50n = 29
$$

### Step 3: Solve for n

$$
8 + 3.50n = 29
$$

Subtract 8 from both sides:

$$
3.50n = 21
$$

Now divide both sides by 3.50:

$$
n = \frac{21}{3.50} = 6
$$

### ✅ Final Answer:
**Sam bought 6 notebooks.**
