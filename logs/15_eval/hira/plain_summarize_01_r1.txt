- The city's first electric tram line opened in 1898, covering 4.2 kilometres between the harbour and the central market.  
- It operated 12 cars, each seating 28 passengers, and used overhead wires at 550 volts.  
- Ridership reached 9,000 passengers a day within two years, surpassing the expected 3,500.  
- By 1925, the network had expanded to 31 kilometres with 6 routes, but faced competition from motor buses.  
- The last tram ran on 14 March 1957, with a 600-metre heritage section restored in 1994 for tourist use.
