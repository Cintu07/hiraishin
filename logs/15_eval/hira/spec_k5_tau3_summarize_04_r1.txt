1898 - The city's first electric tram line opened, running 4.2 kilometres between the harbour and the central market.  
1900 - Ridership reached 9,000 passengers a day, exceeding the expected 3,500.  
1903 - The tram company ordered 18 more cars and extended the line north to the university district.  
1925 - The tram network expanded to 31 kilometres with 6 routes, but faced competition from motor buses.  
1957 - The last tram ran on 14 March, marking the end of the city's electric tram service.  
1994 - A 600-metre heritage section near the market was restored and now carries tourists on weekends.
