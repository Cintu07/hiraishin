We are given:

- **Pipe A** fills the tank in **6 hours**  
- **Pipe B** fills the tank in **9 hours**  
- **Pipe C** drains the tank in **12 hours**  
- All three pipes are opened at **8:00 AM**  
- The tank is **empty** at the start

We are to find **at what time the tank becomes full**.

---

### Step 1: Determine the rates of each pipe

Let’s assume the **total volume of the tank is 1 unit**.

- **Rate of Pipe A** = $ \frac{1}{6} $ tank per hour  
- **Rate of Pipe B** = $ \frac{1}{9} $ tank per hour  
- **Rate of Pipe C** = $ -\frac{1}{12} $ tank per hour (negative because it drains)

---

### Step 2: Combine the rates

Total rate when all three pipes are open:

$$
\text{Total rate} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{9} - \frac{1}{12}
$$

Find a common denominator (LCM of 6, 9, 1
